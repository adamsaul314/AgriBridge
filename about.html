About AgriBridge

Irish-owned, hands-on, and built on real agricultural experience

Who We Are

We've Done It Ourselves

At the heart of AgriBridge is agriculture, and we know that a dynamic industry needs a skilled, adaptable workforce. Irish-owned and operated, our team brings real hands-on experience, having worked on farms and contracting seasons across Ireland, the UK, the USA, and Australia. This experience gives us a unique understanding of the challenges candidates and employers face.

James has 20+ years of experience in the agricultural farming industry and has worked in Ireland, the UK, the USA, Australia, and New Zealand.

We're not afraid to roll up our sleeves — every member of our team plays a vital role in making placements run smoothly, from start to finish.

We believe in making agriculture accessible to young people, giving them the skills, experience, and confidence to take on the next generation of farming. At AgriBridge, we don't just place candidates — we empower them to thrive overseas and in their future careers.

How We Operate

We meticulously select experienced farm workers, focusing on those who have expertise in key areas such as dairy farming, crop harvesting, and machinery operation.

We facilitate the relocation process, ensuring a smooth transition for both workers and the farms they'll be joining — guiding them through visas, IRD numbers, bank accounts, accommodation, and cultural adjustments.

At AgriBridge Recruitment, we are committed to creating long-term connections between skilled farm workers and agricultural sectors around the world, fostering mutual success and growth.

College & University Placements

Supporting Students Nationwide

AgriBridge has helped students and graduates from leading agricultural colleges across Ireland secure paid farming placements abroad. We've successfully supported candidates from:

  • Ballyhaise Agricultural College
  • Mountbellew Agricultural College
  • Kildalton Agricultural College
  • University College Dublin
  • University of Limerick

Through our trusted network of farmers and contractors, we guide candidates through the entire process, from applications and visas to travel, tax numbers, and arrival. We make it easy to gain hands-on agricultural experience overseas.
