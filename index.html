Candidates

Find Work in New Zealand

Working in New Zealand's agricultural industry offers an incredible opportunity to grow your skills while experiencing life abroad. With roles available across the sector including tractor and machinery work, dairy farming, and dry stock farming, New Zealand is the best destination for your next step in agriculture.

With AgriBridge's support, you'll be backed throughout your journey, ensuring a safe, rewarding, and enjoyable working holiday. We provide a complete package that includes a secured job, competitive wages, accommodation, airport collection, and support setting up tax registration.

AgriBridge leads the way in connecting candidates with genuine opportunities delivering hands-on experience, international travel, and memories that last a lifetime.

About AgriBridge

AgriBridge has helped students and graduates from leading agricultural colleges across Ireland secure paid farming placements abroad. We've successfully supported candidates from Ballyhaise Agricultural College, Mountbellew Agricultural College, Kildalton Agricultural College, University College Dublin, and the University of Limerick.

Through our trusted network of farmers and contractors, we guide candidates through the entire process, from applications and visas to travel, tax numbers, and arrival. We make it easy to gain hands-on agricultural experience overseas.
