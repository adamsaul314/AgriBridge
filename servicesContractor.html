Find the Right Staff for You

We understand that sourcing reliable staff can be challenging, and securing access to key talent remains a big concern within the agricultural industry. That's where we come in to help — we have access to top quality staff looking for job opportunities in New Zealand and Australia.

We collaborate with mainly UK and Irish Young Farmers but also collaborate with young farmers from all over europe, Agricultural Universities, and Colleges, leveraging our social media platforms to promote opportunities. Our team of recruitment experts is committed to guiding you throughout the hiring process, ensuring a smooth and efficient experience.

Quality Staffing Solutions

At AgriBridge, we pride ourselves on sourcing the best quality staff for your upcoming harvest. We select candidates from a range of agricultural colleges around Ireland and accept applications from young farmers. Our recruitment process includes vetting, interviewing, and checking references to ensure each candidate is perfectly suited to your needs.

Available Roles

We specialise in filling roles such as farm hands, machinery operators, and farm managers, providing you with the skilled workers you need.

Flexible Contracts

We offer staffing solutions that align with the seasonal nature of agricultural work. Whether you need workers for just a few weeks during harvest season or for a full year, we provide flexible contract lengths. Scale your workforce up or down as required.
